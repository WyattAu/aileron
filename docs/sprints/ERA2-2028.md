## GATE-9 (NEW, blocking scoped-session production use)

**Symptom:** after a burst of tool calls on a *scoped* session (scope to
`[read]`, ~6 reads), the browser's command pipeline stalls: new MCP
commands queue without responses for minutes, then either self-resolves
or is healed by the watchdog restart. Unscoped/default sessions are
unaffected; scoping correctness itself is verified (FORBIDDEN
enforcement, per-session identity, monotone narrowing all proven live).

**Evidence:** log silence at the stall onset with later "Broken pipe"
writes to closed probe sockets (the pipeline caught up after the probe
gave up); same-process recovery within ~10 minutes (0.2s response after
a slow window, no restart).

**Ruled out:** tick panic (catch_unwind logs — zero entries); thread-per-
session starvation (fixed separately: the sequential accept loop starved
long-lived connections — also fixed the relay to hold ONE connection);
GTK re-entrancy (guard added, panic-safe).

**Leading hypotheses:** (a) the φ-gate's registry lock contends with the
per-call κ gate under the 8ms tick; (b) a bounded settle-hold inflating
under scoped sessions (60s waits stacking); (c) event-channel backlog
accumulating while nested ticks are skipped during settle pumps.

**Mitigation:** watchdog restart heals within ≤5min; unscoped usage
(default) unaffected. **Exit criteria:** repro eliminated under a
scoped-session soak; stall window <1s.

**Soak data (2026-10-31, post-fix binary):** 8-minute scoped-session soak
(scope=[read,mutate], navigate+read cycles): 193 cycles, 0 errors, zero
slow (>5s) cycles — the stall did NOT reproduce under sustained scoped
traffic. Classification: intermittent, repro-specific (scoped [read]-only
burst with a forbidden-navigate interleave), watchdog-mitigated. GATE-9
stays open for the targeted repro.
